About the course

Every hotel sells something it cannot store: tonight’s room, this evening’s table, this weekend’s banquet hall. This course teaches how hotels find the right guests, shape an offer they want, price it, tell them about it and keep them coming back. It mixes marketing concepts with practical selling and digital skills, through cases, role plays and a group marketing plan for a real-style hotel.

Units

  1. Unit 1 · Marketing Fundamentals for Hospitality
  2. Unit 2 · Guest Behaviour, Market Research and STP
  3. Unit 3 · The Hospitality Marketing Mix
  4. Unit 4 · Selling and Guest Relationship Skills
  5. Unit 5 · Digital Marketing for Hotels

Assessment

Internal assessment and an end-semester examination as per NCHMCT norms. Internal components: CIA-1 (Units 1–2), CIA-2 (Units 3–4, including a selling role play), mini-project (hotel marketing plan, presented in Session 30) and class participation.

Textbooks

  • Kotler, P., Bowen, J. T., Makens, J. C. & Baloglu, S. Marketing for Hospitality and Tourism. Pearson.
  • Lovelock, C., Wirtz, J. & Chatterjee, J. Services Marketing: People, Technology, Strategy. Pearson.
  • Kotler, P. & Keller, K. L. Marketing Management. Pearson.

Programme Outcomes

  • PO1 Hospitality knowledge: Apply knowledge of hotel and tourism operations and management.
  • PO2 Professional skills: Perform operational and service tasks to industry standards.
  • PO3 Problem solving: Analyse business situations and make sound, evidence-based decisions.
  • PO4 Communication: Communicate clearly and courteously with guests, teams and stakeholders.
  • PO5 Teamwork and leadership: Work effectively in teams and take responsibility for outcomes.
